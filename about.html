Our Story

Founded in 2015 in Bhubaneswar, Electronic World began with a simple vision: to bring the world's best technology to Odisha at the most competitive prices. What started as a single store has grown into a trusted electronics brand with 5 locations across the state, serving over 5,000+ customers with genuine products and exceptional after-sales service.
